Our Products

Product Range

We produce and supply a range of cassava-based products designed for both food and industrial applications.

Garri

Produced via controlled fermentation and thermal processing to achieve microbiological safety, stable moisture content, and uniform granulation.

Cassava Chips

Dried to standardized moisture specifications, suitable for bulk handling, international shipment, and industrial use.

High-Quality Cassava Flour (HQCF)

Gluten-free, finely milled flour meeting consistency and purity requirements for global food and industrial markets.

Fufu Pulp

Fermented and processed for uniformity, ease of packaging, and reliable reconstitution across markets.

Cassava Peels Grits

Processed into stable, nutrient-rich livestock feed input, suitable for large-scale agricultural supply chains.

Our Quality Commitment

Kofo Agro Allied Ltd. operates an integrated quality management system that governs all stages of production, from raw material selection through processing and final packaging. Our processes are designed to ensure product integrity, safety, and batch-to-batch consistency.
