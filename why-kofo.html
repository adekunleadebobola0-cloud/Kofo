Our Strengths

Why Partners Trust Kofo

Integrated Operations

We manage the full cassava value chain from cultivation and sourcing to processing, packaging, and distribution allowing us to maintain consistent quality, efficient production, and reliable supply.

Strong Raw Material Supply

With access to over 250 hectares of cultivated farmland, we ensure a steady supply of high-quality cassava for continuous production and long-term scalability.

Sustainable Processing

Our operations are designed to maximize cassava utilization through efficient milling, drying, and packaging systems that reduce waste and improve production efficiency.

Reliable Supply & Partnerships

We work closely with food processors, manufacturers, distributors, and commercial buyers to provide dependable supply solutions tailored to market demands.

Local Impact

By supporting local farmers through stable demand and agricultural partnerships, we help create jobs, strengthen rural livelihoods, and contribute to sustainable agricultural growth.

Our Quality Commitment

Kofo Agro Allied Ltd. operates an integrated quality management system that governs all stages of production, from raw material selection through processing and final packaging. Our processes are designed to ensure product integrity, safety, and batch-to-batch consistency.

Contact Us

Kofo Agro Allied Ltd. is an integrated cassava processing and manufacturing company specializing in the production, packaging, and distribution of high-quality cassava-based products for food, industrial, and agricultural markets.
