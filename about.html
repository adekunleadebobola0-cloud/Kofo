Who We Are

Kofo Agro Allied Ltd.

Kofo Agro Allied Ltd. is an integrated cassava processing and manufacturing company specializing in the production, packaging, and distribution of high-quality cassava-based products for food, industrial, and agricultural markets.

We operate across the full value chain from raw material sourcing and controlled processing to hygienic packaging and reliable distribution ensuring consistency, quality, and dependable supply for our customers.

What We Do

Transforming Cassava Into Value

We transform cassava into value-added products that serve multiple industries. Our operations are designed to deliver safe, standardized, and high-performance products that meet both local and export market requirements.

Our product portfolio includes cassava derivatives such as garri, cassava chips, high-quality cassava flour (HQCF), fufu pulp, and cassava peels for animal feed.

Each product is processed under controlled conditions to ensure uniformity, safety, and functional quality across every batch.

Our Journey

Our History

Established in 2017, Kofo Agro Allied Ltd. was founded with a clear vision: to transform cassava processing through efficiency, quality, and sustainable sourcing. From the outset, we recognized the importance of controlling our supply chain to maintain consistency and reliability.

To achieve this, we implemented a strong backward integration model, cultivating over 250 hectares of farmland dedicated to cassava production. This investment allows us to ensure a steady supply of raw materials while maintaining superior quality through modern farming techniques.

Over the years, we have grown into a trusted partner for businesses that rely on cassava-based inputs, continuously expanding our capabilities and reinforcing our commitment to innovation, sustainability, and excellence in agro-processing.

Our Quality Commitment

Kofo Agro Allied Ltd. operates an integrated quality management system that governs all stages of production, from raw material selection through processing and final packaging. Our processes are designed to ensure product integrity, safety, and batch-to-batch consistency.
