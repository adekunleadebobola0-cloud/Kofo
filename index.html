About Us

Who We Are

Kofo Agro Allied Ltd. is an integrated cassava processing and manufacturing company specializing in the production, packaging, and distribution of high-quality cassava-based products for food, industrial, and agricultural markets.

  • Over 250 hectares of cultivated farmland dedicated to cassava production
  • Full value chain from raw material sourcing to reliable distribution
  • Integrated quality management system governing all stages of production

Our Products

Product and Services

We produce and supply a range of cassava-based products designed for both food and industrial applications.

Garri

Produced via controlled fermentation and thermal processing to achieve microbiological safety, stable moisture content, and uniform granulation.

Cassava Chips

Dried to standardized moisture specifications, suitable for bulk handling, international shipment, and industrial use.

High-Quality Cassava Flour (HQCF)

Gluten-free, finely milled flour meeting consistency and purity requirements for global food and industrial markets.
